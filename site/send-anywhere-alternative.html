vrok / vrok vs Send Anywhere and WeTransfer

A free Send Anywhere and WeTransfer alternative

Send Anywhere and WeTransfer put your files on their servers, then cap how much you can send for free. vrok serves the files straight from your computer instead, so there is no size limit, no monthly cap and no upload to wait for. It is free, open source and needs no account.

Why people switch to vrok

  • No size limit. A 50 GB folder works like a 5 MB PDF. The only limit is your disk.
  • No monthly cap. Send as many files, as often as you like. There is no transfer count and no download quota that runs out halfway through the month.
  • No upload wait. The link works the moment you run the command, and the other person starts downloading straight away.
  • Free for good. MIT licensed, no paid tier, no ads, no telemetry. Nothing is held back for a subscription.
  • No copy on someone else's server. Your files stay on your machine. When the link ends, there is nothing left anywhere to delete.
  • You set the expiry. Ten minutes, two days, one download, a password, or until you press Ctrl+C. You can change it while the share is running.
  • Better for the recipient. Videos, images, PDFs and folders preview in the browser, interrupted downloads resume, a folder downloads as one zip, and a file page shows its SHA-256 so they can check their copy.
  • It also shares localhost. The same tool gives your dev server a public URL with vrok localhost:3000.

Send a file in two steps

  1. Install vrok with one command, Homebrew, Scoop or a package from the download page.
  2. Run vrok ./video.mp4, or a folder, and send the link. The URL is copied for you.

Add --ttl 2h to close it after two hours, --downloads 1 for a one-time link, or --password to lock it.

When Send Anywhere or WeTransfer fits better

  • The recipient must be able to download after your computer is switched off. vrok serves from your machine, so the link works only while it runs.
  • You want an app with buttons, not a terminal. vrok is a command-line tool.
  • Your upload speed is very slow and the recipient is in no hurry. With vrok, their download runs at your connection's upload speed.

Questions

Is vrok a free alternative to Send Anywhere?

Yes. vrok is free and open source (MIT) with no paid tier. Its links have no size limit and no monthly download cap, because the files are served from your own computer instead of a company's servers.

Is vrok a free alternative to WeTransfer?

For sending files while your computer is on, yes. There is no 3 GB limit, no limit on how many transfers you send, and nothing to upload first. If the recipient must download after you switch off, a file host like WeTransfer fits better.

Does the recipient need an app or an account?

No. They open the link in any browser, on a computer or a phone. On a phone they can scan the QR code vrok prints instead of typing the link. Only the sender runs vrok.

How fast is it?

The download starts the moment you send the link, with no upload step. The speed is limited by your internet upload speed and theirs, and an interrupted download resumes where it stopped.

Install vrok

One command on macOS and Linux. Free, MIT licensed, no account.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AliJabbar034/vrok/main/install.sh | sh

Homebrew, Scoop, Windows, Linux packages and verified archives are on the download page. Every command and flag is in the guide.
